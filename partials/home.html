The Value of Money

Money plays a major role in shaping the way people live. It determines access to education, housing, healthcare, and overall quality of life. While money itself is not the ultimate goal, it provides the means to reach personal and professional ambitions.

However, the value of money is not only about how much someone has. It also depends on how it is managed. Good financial habits such as saving, budgeting, and investing can create long-term stability, while poor decisions can lead to stress and insecurity.

In my opinion, money should be seen as a tool rather than a definition of success. It is important, but it should support happiness and growth instead of controlling it.

GMO Food

Genetically modified organisms, or GMOs, are foods that have been scientifically altered to improve certain characteristics such as resistance to pests or longer shelf life. According to the FDA, many GMO crops are used in ingredients found in everyday foods, while a large portion is also used for animal feed.

Supporters believe GMO foods can help reduce crop losses and increase food production. Examples include crops designed to resist insects or to stay fresh longer. These innovations can help agriculture respond to the needs of a growing population.

In my opinion, GMO foods are not entirely good or bad. They represent progress, but they also require transparency and careful regulation. People should be informed about what they consume and how it is produced.

Source: U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), GMO Crops, Animal Food, and Beyond.

The View of the World in 2100

By the year 2100, the world could be transformed by rapid technological advancement. Cities may become smarter, transportation faster, and communication almost instant. Artificial intelligence and automation could redefine the way people live and work.

Despite these advancements, the future will also bring challenges. Issues such as climate change, inequality, and the ethical use of technology will remain central concerns. Progress does not guarantee fairness or sustainability.

I believe the world in 2100 will depend on the decisions we make today. Technology will shape the future, but human values will determine whether that future benefits everyone.
